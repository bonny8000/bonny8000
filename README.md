# Bonny

**Product Designer | UX Research | AI-Enabled Design Workflows**

I design repeatable systems that help teams move from ambiguous product inputs to clear, evidence-backed decisions and deliverables. My work spans product design, UX research, information architecture, motion, and AI-assisted workflow design.

## Areas of Focus

- **Product discovery and research synthesis** — turning qualitative evidence into traceable findings and actionable direction
- **Information architecture and interaction design** — defining user stories, flows, edge cases, navigation, and wireframes
- **Design systems** — converting design judgment into reusable patterns, documented rules, and maintainable components
- **AI workflow design** — building agent skills with explicit inputs, quality gates, validation, and reviewable outputs
- **Design communication** — explaining complex product decisions through structured presentations and motion

## Selected Work

### [Bonny Slide System](https://github.com/bonny8000/bonny-slide-generate-system)

A design system and agent workflow for producing consistent UX and product presentations in Traditional Chinese and English. It combines a catalogued layout library, reusable components, content routing, themes, validation, and PDF export.

### [Interview Analysis Skill](https://github.com/bonny8000/Interview-Analysis-Skill)

An evidence-led qualitative research workflow that transforms raw interview transcripts into traceable coding, themes, counter-evidence, and actionable insights without losing source context.

### [Motion Skill](https://github.com/bonny8000/Motion-Skill)

A system for designing short product animations and motion concepts using reusable stages, shapes, and beats, with seekable HTML timelines, layout audits, and verified video export.

### Product Design Toolkit

A connected set of methods for moving from product intent to structure and design:

- [User Story Skill](https://github.com/bonny8000/User-Story-Skill) — roles, context, motivation, outcomes, and failure modes
- [User Flow & Edge Cases Skill](https://github.com/bonny8000/User-Flow-Edge-Cases-Skill) — steps, states, decisions, exceptions, and recovery paths
- [Sitemap Generate](https://github.com/bonny8000/Sitemap-Generate) — information architecture and page hierarchy derived from product requirements
- [Wireframe Skill](https://github.com/bonny8000/Wireframe-Skill) — structured wireframes that make product decisions visible and reviewable
